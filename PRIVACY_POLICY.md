## 1. Introduction
Image to PDF Converter is developed by **AP Programming Tech** as a completely free, open-source, and offline utility. We are deeply committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This policy explains our data practices for the application.

---

## 2. Zero Data Collection
Image to PDF Converter does **NOT** collect, record, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or sensitive user data. 

Specifically:
- **No Account Required**: The application requires no user registration, sign-up, or login.
- **No Personal Information**: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts.
- **No Analytics / Tracking**: We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or behavioral trackers.
- **No Identifiers**: We do not collect device IDs, advertising IDs (AAID), or IP addresses.

---

## 3. 100% Offline & Local On-Device Processing
- All image conversions, rotations, cropping, compression, and PDF document creations occur **entirely on your local device CPU**.
- Your images, photos, and generated PDF files **never leave your device** and are never transmitted to any external server or cloud service.
- The application does not require and does not request internet access (`android.permission.INTERNET` is omitted from the application manifest).

---

## 4. Device Permissions
The application requests only the minimum device permissions strictly necessary for offline document creation:
- **Photos / Media / Storage**: Used exclusively to allow you to select images from your local gallery and save the generated PDF file to your device storage.
- **Camera (Optional)**: Used exclusively if you choose to take a photo of a document within the app.

No files, images, or camera feeds are ever uploaded or shared outside your device.

---

## 5. Advertisements & Monetization
- **Zero Advertisements**: The application does not contain Google AdMob, banner ads, interstitial ads, or any advertising network SDKs.
- **Completely Free**: No subscription fees, paywalls, or in-app purchases.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy
Because Image to PDF Converter does not collect any personal data whatsoever, it is safe for all audiences, including children under the age of 13.
